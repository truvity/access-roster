This is the target state, not a running system. This page shows the identity module on the shared foundation (store@, exporter@ and key@); [the audit explorer](/architecture/audit-explorer/) shows the audit module on the same foundation. The map is drawn from `site/src/data/explorer/architecture.yaml`. Which actors exist, their families, their calls and their key purposes are the [actor registry](/reference/actors/)'s and the [key registry](/reference/keys/)'s; the data file adds the drawing, the flows and the installations, written by hand from the design. What each actor and driver is and does is on [Components](/architecture/components/); the words are on [Families](/concepts/families/) and [Data](/concepts/data/).

The map draws three of the four [tested installations](/decisions/0011-runtimes-and-installations/#installations): local, boards and lambda. It does not draw the [Kubernetes installation](/runtimes/kubernetes/) yet. Pick an installation, and the map, the tables and the flows follow it. Without script the page shows boards, and every table is plain HTML.

## The map

Select a box to see what it holds, its rules and key purposes in the registries, and its listeners; the lines show who it calls and who calls it. Choose a flow to step through it. A chip says whether a point is decided or proposed, and a proposed point links its tracking issue.

## Clusters

On the boards installation each board runs the full set of units over one rqlite cluster. The rule behind the writer row is on [Audit](/concepts/audit/), what a board carries under [Board capacity](/runtimes/bare-metal/#board-capacity), and which card slot holds which key in [the slot plan](/decisions/0006-keys-and-sealing/#the-slot-plan).
